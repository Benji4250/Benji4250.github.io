Dashboard

Dashboard is a private, self-hosted status screen for a small business. It runs on a single computer owned by the business and is used only by its owner.

With the owner's permission, it shows the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail messages on that screen, and lets the owner read, reply to, archive, and delete those messages from it.

It is not offered to the public and does not accept new users.
