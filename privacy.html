Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Who uses this app

Dashboard is a private app used only by its owner on a computer the owner controls. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: the owner's messages, to show them on the dashboard and to let the owner read, send, reply to, archive, and delete messages, and to recognize shipping emails for delivery tracking.
  • Google Calendar: the owner's events, to show upcoming events, and to add or change events when the owner asks.

Where data goes

  • Data from Google is processed on the owner's own computer and shown only on the owner's dashboard and the owner's phone over a private network.
  • It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Short alert messages (for example "Out for delivery") may be sent to the owner's phone through the ntfy notification service. They never include account passwords or sign-in details.
  • Sign-in tokens from Google are stored only on the owner's computer, in a folder only that computer's administrators can read.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Dashboard at any time in the dashboard's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored sign-in tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
